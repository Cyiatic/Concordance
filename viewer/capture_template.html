Capture in three deliberate passes

The adapter reads only messages Discord has visibly rendered in the open DM. Repeat the loop at each boundary and across long gaps.

1
Navigate manually

Open the intended DM in Discord and confirm the visible channel URL before running the adapter.

2
Settle and capture

Scroll older or newer, wait for messages and media to finish rendering, then save the JSON range.

3
Add and inspect

Add the range here, check overlap and checkpoints, then repeat until both boundaries are attested.

iUse overlap on purpose. Each adjacent range should share at least one rendered message. A range that merely “looks next” is not enough to prove continuity.

honest limits

What this can and cannot prove

It can prove: which visible ranges were recorded, their overlap, timestamps, IDs, boundaries, and captured feature references.
It cannot prove: messages Discord did not render, messages deleted before capture, or content hidden behind an unloaded media/embed state.
Rendered proof: save a DOM snapshot or screenshot during the attended browser step, then attach it to its range before exporting evidence.
Fresh chats: do not guess dates. Start at the newest visible end, capture overlapping ranges while walking older, then confirm both ends.
